# Monica & Nathaniel Villegas

A private, mobile-first invitation for the civil ceremony, served at `https://natemon.com/civil/`. Guest names stay in a Google Sheet on your personal account. Each household gets a unique link.

The site root, `index.html`, is a placeholder for the future website. It does not link to `/civil/`, and the invitation should stay out of any future navigation. The invitation also asks search engines not to index it.

Open `civil/index.html` in Safari to preview the invitation before the sheet is connected.

- Couple preview: `civil/index.html`
- Single-guest preview: `civil/index.html?preview=single`
- Sample menu and meal questions: `civil/index.html?menu=sample`

Sections fade in as guests scroll. Watercolor illustrations in `civil/assets/` sit between sections: a hot air balloon, City Hall, the Bay Bridge, and mistletoe.

Replies are not saved until `scriptUrl` in `civil/config.js` is filled in. Setup is in `SETUP.md`.
